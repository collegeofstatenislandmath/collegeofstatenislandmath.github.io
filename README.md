# CSI Mathematics Department Website

This repository hosts the official website for the ***Department of Mathematics at the College of Staten Island (CSI), CUNY***. 

## About

The CSI Mathematics Department website provides information about:

- **Academic Programs**: Bachelor of Science degrees with emphases on graduate school preparation, secondary school teaching, and a joint degree with computer science
- **Faculty Research**: Information about our ~25 full-time faculty members and their research in areas including:
  - Applied mathematics
  - Differential geometry
  - Discrete mathematics
  - Geometric analysis
  - Hyperbolic conservation laws
  - Knot theory
  - Logic
  - Nonlinear analysis
  - Number theory
  - Probability
  - Topology
- **Course Offerings**: Full list of mathematics courses for mathematics, engineering, and science students, as well as service courses
- **News & Events**: Department announcements, seminars, and upcoming events
- **Undergraduate Research**: Information about faculty-mentored research opportunities
